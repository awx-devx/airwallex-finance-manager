# Spending policy (example)

Replace this file with your real policy. The agent should treat it as guidance, not as permission to move money.

- Cards: individual monthly limit unless Finance raises it.
- New vendors over $5,000 need a named owner before payment is prepared.
- Recurring SaaS over $1,000 / month needs a category owner.
- Money-out actions are prepare-only until an allowlisted operator replies YES in Telegram.
- Do not pay a new beneficiary and a first invoice in the same unconfirmed turn.
